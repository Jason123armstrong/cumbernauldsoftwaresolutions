Your whole round, in your pocket.

We make Golf Score & More, a fast, friendly Android scorecard. It shows GPS distances to the green, puts your whole group on one live card and runs a leaderboard for society days, all from one app.

Why golfers use it

Everything for the round, nothing in the way

Big, clear buttons and scores entered in a single tap, so you spend your time on the golf, not the phone.

One-tap scoring

Up to four players, gross and net, putts, fairways and greens. Stroke play, Stableford, skins and more.

GPS distances

Front, middle and back of the green, bunker carries, and a "plays like" yardage that allows for wind, temperature and slope.

Live group card

Share one card with your playing partners by QR code. Every phone sees the same scores, phone to phone, without needing internet.

Competitions

Run a society day or club competition with a six-letter code and one live leaderboard for every group on the course.

Scan a paper card

Photograph or upload a paper scorecard and the scores are read for you, so old rounds join your history too.

Analysis and handicap

Trends, putting and clubs, plus an estimated handicap index worked out the World Handicap System way.

On the course

Know the number before you pull a club

Turn on Follow me and the hole map shows exactly where you are, with live distances to the green and every hazard in front of you.

  • Front, middle and back of the green, in yards or metres
  • Distances to reach and carry each bunker
  • "Plays like" yardage allowing for wind, temperature and height
  • Sun mode: bright, high-contrast colours, with the screen kept on
Every shot counted

Scoring that keeps up with your group

Tap a number and you're done. The card keeps running totals, net scores and standings while you walk to the next tee.

  • Birdies, bogeys and handicap strokes marked the traditional way
  • A score-against-par chart for every player
  • Optional putts, tee shots and approach notes for deeper stats
How it works

On the first tee in under a minute

Find your course

Tap Courses near me. The app finds golf courses within 25 km and downloads each hole's par and stroke index.

Add your players

Pick up to four players. Their handicaps are remembered, so next time it's one tap.

Tee off

Score each hole as you go, check distances on the map and share the card live with your group.
