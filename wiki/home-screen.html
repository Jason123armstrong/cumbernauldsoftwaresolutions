The Home screen

  • Continue round: shown when a round is in progress, with the course and hole you're on.
  • Start a round: set up a new round. Guide.
  • Find a local club: nearby courses, cards and prices. Guide.
  • Competitions: organise or join one live leaderboard. Guide.
  • ❤ at the top opens health stats, and 💡 lets you suggest an improvement.

The bar along the bottom

Home, Scorecard (the Scorecard+ menu), Competitions, Clubs and More. Tap any of them to jump straight there.

The Scorecard+ menu

Play

  • Continue round and New round (up to 4 players, any game).
  • Competitions, Join a group's live card and Scan a paper scorecard.

Records

  • Round history, Friends, Analysis, Players and Courses. The numbers on the right show how many you have saved.

Settings, data and help

  • Options: display, sounds, game defaults, what to record and cloud backup.
  • Save scorecards to Downloads, Ideas and feedback and Report a problem.

Buttons on every screen

  • ← goes back one screen. The phone's back gesture does the same.
  • ⋮ opens Suggest an improvement and Report a problem for the screen you're on.
  • ✕ closes the screen. Your scores are already saved.
