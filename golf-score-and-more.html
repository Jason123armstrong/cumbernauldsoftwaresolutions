Features

Built around the scorecard

Scorecard+ is the heart of the app. Everything else is there to make the round quicker, fairer or more fun.

Score a round in seconds

Start a new round, pick your course and up to four players, and tee off. Each hole is one tap per player.

  • Standard mode: stroke play with gross and net scores, putts, fairways and greens in regulation
  • Fun mode: Stableford points, skins, birdie celebrations and end-of-round awards
  • Front nine, back nine or the full eighteen
  • Tap any hole on the card to correct it

How to score a hole →

Distances you can trust

Course maps come from OpenStreetMap. Turn on Follow me to see where you are and how far it is.

  • Front, middle and back of the green
  • Reach and carry distances for bunkers
  • "Plays like" yardage allowing for wind, temperature and slope
  • Sun mode for bright days

Using the hole map →

Find a local club

Tap Find a local club and the app lists every golf course within 25 km, so you can compare them before you book.

  • Distance from you, nearest first
  • Approximate visitor prices and offers, read from each club's website
  • Course rating and slope for the tees you play
  • Sort by nearest, cheapest, offers or A–Z
  • Save a course to download its scorecard, holes and map

Prices are approximate and change with the day and season. Always check with the club before booking.

Finding local courses →

A proper scorecard

The full card uses the marks golfers know: circles for birdies, squares for bogeys and dots for handicap strokes.

  • Live standings for stroke play, net or Stableford
  • A score-against-par chart for each player
  • Copies saved to your phone's Downloads, including a spreadsheet file
