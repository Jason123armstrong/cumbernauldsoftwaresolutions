Golf Score & More

A golf app for Android that keeps your score, shows distances on the course and makes it easy to play with friends.

Features

Scorecards

Record every hole quickly, see your totals as you play and keep a history of your rounds.

Distances

Use your phone's location to see how far you are from the green.

Courses near you

Find golf courses close to where you are.

Play with friends

Share scores and play rounds together with optional cloud features.

Your privacy

Your rounds are stored on your phone. Cloud features are off until you turn them on. Read our privacy policy for full details.
