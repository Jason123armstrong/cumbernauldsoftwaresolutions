About us

A small company that builds and supports its own apps

Cumbernauld Software Solutions Limited is an independent software company based in Cumbernauld, Scotland. We design, build and support our own mobile apps: Golf Score & More for golfers, and Chessosaurus, dinosaur chess for children.

We build what our users ask for. Every feature request and problem report is read by the people who write the code.

Privacy comes first: we keep your data on your device unless you choose otherwise, and explain everything in plain English in our privacy policy.
