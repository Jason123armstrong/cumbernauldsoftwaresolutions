1. On the Scorecard+ menu, tap Round history. The number beside it is how many rounds you've saved.
  2. Tap a round to open its full card, standings, stats and awards.
  3. Use Share the result to send who won by WhatsApp, text or email, or share a picture of the medal stand and scorecard.

Awards

At the end of a round the app hands out awards, such as Birdie machine, Par collector, Shot of the day, Hot putter and Skins king. A hole in one gets its own award (drinks are on them).

Correcting or deleting a round

  • Correct a score: reopen the card, tap the hole and fix the mistake.
  • Delete this round: removes the round and all its scores. The app asks twice. Any copy already saved in Downloads is kept.
