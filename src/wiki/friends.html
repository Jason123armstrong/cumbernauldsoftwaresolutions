1. On the Scorecard+ menu, tap 👥 Friends.
  2. Next to This is me, tap Choose and pick which player is you. (If you're not listed, add yourself first in Players.)
  3. Turn on Share my finished rounds with friends. You'll get your own friend code and QR code to give to friends.
  4. To follow someone, tap Add a friend and scan their QR code or type their 6-letter code.

Who sees what

  • You see the latest rounds of the friends you add: course, score and points.
  • They see yours only if they add your code. Adding someone doesn't share your rounds with them.
  • Remove a friend at any time and you'll stop seeing their rounds.

Friends uses the cloud, so you'll need signal to add friends and see new rounds.
