1. From any hole, tap Card.
  2. The table shows each hole's par and index, then every player's scores. Swipe sideways to see all 18 holes.
  3. Below the table are the standings for your format, then stats and a score against par chart.
  4. To fix a mistake, tap the hole on the card and enter the right score.

Scorecard symbols

  • ○ Birdie
  • ◎ Eagle or better
  • □ Bogey
  • ▣ Double bogey or worse
  • • Handicap stroke received on that hole

The chart

The score-against-par chart has a line for each player. Touch and drag along it to read the score after any hole.

Sharing the result

After the round you can share the result by WhatsApp, text or email (who won and the standings), or share a picture with the medal stand and full scorecard for social media.
