What you need

  • An Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer.
  • Location turned on if you want course finding and GPS distances.
  • Mobile data or Wi-Fi to download courses. Scoring itself works without signal.

Install the app

Golf Score & More is coming soon to Google Play. When it's available, search for Golf Score & More in the Play Store and tap Install.

Set up before your first round

  1. Open the app. The Home screen has everything you need to start. Take a tour.
  2. Tap Find a local club (or Clubs in the bottom bar). Allow location when asked, then pick your course to save it and download its holes. More about courses.
  3. In Scorecard › Players, add yourself with your handicap index, plus the people you usually play with. They're remembered for next time. More about players.
  4. Have a look at Options to set text size, units, sounds, what to record and whether to use cloud backup. More about options.
  5. When you're on the first tee, tap Start a round. More about starting a round.

Permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it. If you say no, everything else still works.

  • Location: courses near you, Follow me and live distances.
  • Camera: photographing a paper scorecard or a player's picture, and scanning QR codes.
  • Nearby devices: the live group card, which links phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.
  • Microphone: voice control, if you choose to use it.
Tip: scores are saved on your phone as you enter them, so it's safe to close the app mid-round. Continue round on the Home screen takes you straight back to the hole you were on.
