Game formats at a glance

Choose the format when you start a new round. Handicap strokes are applied automatically for net formats.

  • Stroke play and Stroke play (net): fewest strokes wins.
  • Stableford: points per hole against net par (2 for par, 3 for birdie and so on).
  • Match play: two players, hole by hole.
  • Skins: win a hole outright to take its skin; ties carry over.
  • Par / bogey: each hole against net par, +1 win, 0 halve, −1 loss.
  • Nassau: front nine, back nine and the full round, a point each.
  • Better ball (fourball), Vegas and Wolf: games for four players.
  • Scramble (teams): enter each team as one player with a team handicap.
